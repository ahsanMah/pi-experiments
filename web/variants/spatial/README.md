# Spatial

The canvas is the workspace. Instead of a chat pane next to an overview of message nodes, every branch is a card (a small chat window) on an infinite canvas, and a curved wire runs from the exact message a branch forked at to the child's card. You read, reply, steer and branch right on the cards. Focusing a card grows it into a full chat panel beside the canvas (full screen on phones), so the other branches stay visible and keep streaming next to it. Shared context lives on the canvas too: every shared note is a sticky in a lane above the cards, with dashed connectors to the branches whose models see it. How much a card shows depends on the zoom level: far out you see posters with titles and status, at mid zoom the recent messages and the latest answer, and close up the cards in view get live views with the full rendered answer.

## Interactions

- **Cards**: each card has a header (branch color, title, shared-note count, model), a "from *parent* · after *message*" strip, the recent messages (older ones fold into "N earlier"; fork points always stay), the latest answer (it streams live while the branch works), and an inline composer. Drag a card by its header; positions are remembered per viewer, and the Tidy tool resets them.
- **Focus**: double-click a card, press its ⤢ button, press Enter on the current card, or tap a card on a phone. The panel grows out of the card, and the camera centers the card in the space left over. The panel has the full transcript with Branch, Edit & branch and Share on every message, plus rename (click the title), the per-branch model picker, branch-from-tip and share. Esc goes back.
- **Branch**: hover a message row on a card (or a message in the panel) and choose Branch, or Edit & branch on a user message (it forks before the message and puts its text into the new card's composer). The new card slides in next to the card that owns the fork message, its wire draws itself from that row, and the camera follows it.
- **While busy**: the card glows and its dot pulses, a spark travels down the wire into it, and the answer block streams. Its composer turns into Steer, Queue (Enter) and Stop, with Alt+Enter to steer, on the card and in the panel alike.
- **Stickies**: press N or double-click empty canvas to write a draft sticky. Drag it onto a card to share it with that branch (the card lights up as a drop target), or press "All" to share it with every branch. Drag an existing sticky onto another card to add that branch to its audience. Open a sticky to read all of it, change who sees it, or unshare it. Hovering a sticky highlights its connectors (notes shared with every branch show theirs only on hover).
- **Share from a branch**: drag a card's ⊶ knob (right edge) onto another card, or use Share on a message or in the panel. The sheet offers Summary, Transcript or Note and the target branches (or every branch). A summary appears as a shimmering placeholder sticky until the model is done.
- **Navigation**: drag or Space+drag to pan, scroll to pan, Ctrl/⌘+scroll or pinch to zoom. F fits everything, Tab and Shift+Tab cycle cards in tree order, B branches from the latest message, / types into the current card, + − 0 zoom, and ? lists all of this. There is a minimap, a toolbar for zoom and fit, a "Thread" button for a new root conversation, and a theme toggle (system, light or dark; an explicit `data-theme` on `<html>` always wins).

## Trade-offs against the current canvas UI

- **Branches, not messages, are the unit.** The current overview draws every message as a node, which shows the full tree shape but makes a long thread a long chain. Here a thread is one card and only fork points are drawn as wires, so the canvas stays small and readable. Message-level structure inside a branch is condensed: cards list recent messages and fold older ones (fork points always stay visible), and the full transcript is in the focus panel.
- **One place instead of three.** There is no sidebar or separate chat pane: switching branches, replying and sharing all happen where the branch sits. The cost is that reading at length needs the focus panel, and on small screens the canvas and the panel take turns full screen instead of sitting side by side.
- **Shared context is visible.** Notes are objects with wires to their audience rather than a list in the sidebar, and re-targeting is drag and drop. The protocol has no "update share", so changing a note's audience re-shares it and unshares the old one; the original targets get a second "shared" marker in their transcripts.
- **Live data scales with attention.** Cards use tree previews by default and get live views only when focused, current, working, or in view at reading zoom (up to 8 at a time). Far out, the canvas works as a map; close up, every card in view shows its answer in full.
- **Layout is automatic, with manual overrides.** A left-to-right tidy tree places each child near its fork row so wires run level; dragged positions are stored per viewer in `localStorage` (keyed by conversation id) and may need a Tidy after switching to a different database.
